ABOUT IDEA TRADE

Requirement-Based Sourcing. Practical Procurement Support.

IDEA TRADE focuses on sourcing, procurement coordination, and product resale opportunities for commercial organizations, institutions, and public-sector buyers. Our approach begins with the buyer’s actual requirement — product specifications, quantities, commercial objectives, and delivery needs — and builds the sourcing process around those parameters.
