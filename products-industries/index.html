HOW TO REQUEST A PRODUCT

Send Us What You Need

You do not need to find the right supplier before contacting IDEA TRADE. Start with the product requirement.

Send a product name, part number, specification, drawing, link, photo, parts list, or other available information.

Input format: Any available specs
STEP 02 local_shipping

Define Quantity & Delivery

Include required quantities, target delivery location, timing, and any relevant purchasing requirements.

Parameters: Volume & destinations
STEP 03 manage_search

Potential suppliers and available product options are reviewed against the provided requirement.
