Institutional Clarification

IDEA TRADE operates as an independent sourcing and procurement coordinator. IDEA TRADE makes no claims of guaranteed bid compliance, guaranteed contract awards, regulatory compliance certifications, government agency schedule representation (such as GSA), or guaranteed delivery and fixed pricing until formal written quotation acceptance.
